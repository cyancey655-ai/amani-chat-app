Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Chat content: your conversations with AI companions, stored so you can continue them later.
  • Billing info: plan status and metered usage. Card details are handled directly by Stripe — we never see or store your card number.

How we use it

To run the service: authenticate you, keep your chat history, and bill you correctly. We do not sell your personal data.

AI processing

Chat messages are sent to our AI provider to generate replies. Do not share sensitive personal information (full address, financial details, passwords) in chat.

Your rights

You can request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by contacting support. Deleting your account removes your conversations and usage records.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and a local browser flag for the 18+ age gate. Nothing else.
